# Aria — Voice Command Guide

**Aria by CSVRStudios · www.csvrstudios.com**

Say **"Aria"**, wait for the chime and glow, then speak. After she answers she
keeps listening for a couple of seconds, so you can keep going without saying
her name again. **Alt+0** stops anything instantly (autopilot, auto-clicker,
playback).

These are examples — Aria understands natural phrasing, so "turn it down,"
"lower the volume," and "make it quieter" all work the same.

- "Aria, click the red button" (finds it on screen by description)

- "Aria, record a macro" -> do your combo -> "stop recording, call it fireball"
- "Aria, play fireball" / "list my macros"

Autopilot is a vision model reading your screen about once a second — fine for
exploring and menus, poor at reflexes, and it'll lose fights. Use **Borderless
Windowed**, single-player only. If input doesn't register, run Aria as
administrator.

## What Aria Won't Do
She cannot delete, move, rename, or overwrite files — that ability doesn't exist
in the code, so it can't happen by accident or a misheard word. She can find and
open files, and that's the limit. Irreversible Discord actions (kick, ban, delete
role, delete channel, purge) always confirm first. She does not report IP
addresses.
